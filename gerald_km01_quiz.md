### Question 5

Explain the SIX phases of the System Development Life Cycle (SDLC). (12)

System Planning is the process of identifying system objectives, mapping out and gathering software requirements as per the demands of the system's needs, scope and boundaries.Here,  requirements gathering is done to identify exactly what the system needs and also evaluating whether a project is economically, technically and operationally feasible.


System Analysis is the process of examining, collecting and interpreting facts, breaking down a system into it's core componets and identifiying the system problems as per the requirements of the system's needs in order to identify it's inefficiencies and determine the best optimal solutions to solve them.Here, investigations are done to identify what exactly is the problem that needs to be solved,analyze how to use the collected data to come up with a solution and the requiremnets needed.


System Design is the process of templating a system's architecture, components, interfaces and data flow in order to meet it's functional and non-functional requirements. System design helps with identifying a system's target users, making it more efficient and reliable.It also factors in scalability as system's that are relaible must be scalable and perform their functional rquirements to the best of their capabilities.

System Implementation is the process of developing a system into actuality by writing the code and configuring the neccessary hardware for the system.This process involves testing the code once written, checking for vulnerabilities that may be caused by bugs in the code and ensuring the perfomance of the system meets the requirements, be they functional or non-functional.

System Deployment is the process of releasing a system so that it becomes available to the organization/client or putting it up in a live environment where it becomes accessible  to end users.Here,a system must have met all it's functional and non-functional requirements for example security and being user intuitive.

System Maintenence is the ongoing process of updating and repairing a system's source code so that it remains effcient, secure and maintains maximum perfomance. This process also includes adding new features that enhance the overall system's perfomance.

### Question 7

A college wants to develop a new online student registration system. Before development begins, management requests a feasibility study.

Discuss the following types of feasibility in relation to the project:

* Operational Feasibility
* Technical Feasibility
* Economic Feasibility
* Schedule Feasibility
* Cultural Feasibility

For the Operational Feasibility of the onlinestudent registrtation system, we have to consider whether the system will actually work in the college's environment.Hence, we must assess if there is a reliable internet access for the students to actually access the online student registration system.We must also take into consideration whether the staff need training to be able to operate the system and whether or not there is IT support to repair and update the system when needed.Another operational procedure to take into consideration is whether the manual registration will be transferred to the online system smoothly so that there is no disruption of the registration process.

Technical feasibility will take into consideration whether or not  there is technology to build the system and the skills and personnel required.The college has to evaluate if they have good developers  who can build a secure web-based online registration system, if they can afford website hosting  and a database to hold all the student information.

Schedule feasibility takes factors that have to with the timeline of duration of the online registration system.Here, there must be a realistic deadline whereby the online registration system must be completed while registration is not taking place.Hence, they must determine if they can build the system in time before the next registration cycle takes place.Use of sprints whereby the developement team has their tasks seperated and scheduled into a certain timeframe will help in developing the online registration system quickly, making every developer responsible.

For the economic feasibility the system will consider if the online  registration  is worth developing or not. The costs would include the wesite hosting, maintenence costs and development cost whereas the profitable perks of the system would be reduced manual paperwork, faster registration processing and fewer errors. 


The online registration system  must be culturally feasible also.This means that the system may face challenges in the sense that some staff might have a hard time adapting to an online registration system.Older students might also struggle, but however, with proper training for both students and staff, the online registration system would prove to be more likely beneficial as it upscales registration time, reducdes room for errors, and ensures records entered are not easily lost as in comparison to manual systems.
